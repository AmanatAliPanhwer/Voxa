VOXA — insertion loop prototype

The decided insert-into-the-frontmost-app loop (clipboard snapshot → write clean text → one paste chord → restore), runnable by hand. The question this answers: does the recovers-from-everything contract hold once you push it through terminals, clipboard-only tiers, and undetectable failures? It is a prototype — it simulates, it does not paste.
